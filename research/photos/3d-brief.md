# 3D image brief: Doomsday Dossier

What to generate for each of the 29 characters, so the site can give every one of them real depth:
the character standing in front of their name, turning as a 3D particle figure, and moving through
their own world on a separate layer.

## Where to put them

```
DIAMOND DOCS\MARVEL PHOTOS\3D\<CHARACTER>\hero.png
DIAMOND DOCS\MARVEL PHOTOS\3D\<CHARACTER>\portrait.png
DIAMOND DOCS\MARVEL PHOTOS\3D\<CHARACTER>\scene.png
DIAMOND DOCS\MARVEL PHOTOS\3D\<CHARACTER>\plate.png
DIAMOND DOCS\MARVEL PHOTOS\3D\<CHARACTER>\loop.mp4      (optional)
```

The 29 folders are already made. Use exactly these file names. PNG, or JPG at the highest quality.
Do them in any order; the site picks up whatever is there each time I rebuild.

## The four images per character

| File | What | Size (minimum) | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **hero.png** (most important) | The character from the knees or mid-thigh up, standing, facing the camera or turned three-quarters, in a strong pose. Centred. Top of the head about a fifth of the way down. The body runs off the bottom edge. Nothing else in the frame. | 4:5 portrait, **3072 × 3840** (at least 2048 × 2560) | Standing in front of their giant name on their file, team page and quiz result |
| **portrait.png** | Head and shoulders, looking at the camera or just past it, eyes about a third of the way down. | 1:1, **3072 × 3072** | The 3D particle figure and the close-up lens |
| **scene.png** | The character in their world (see the table), off to the left or right third, cinematic. | 16:9, **3840 × 2160** | Exhibit prints, the next-file window, the cloth posters |
| **plate.png** | **The same scene, same framing, with the character removed**: the empty background only. | 16:9, 3840 × 2160 | The depth layer: the world and the character move separately as you scroll (the "4D" feel) |

**loop.mp4 (optional, the real 4D):** 4 to 6 seconds of the hero shot that loops without a jump:
breathing, cape or hair moving, a flicker of power. No camera move, no cuts. **Pure black
background (#000000)** so the site can blend it over the name. 1080 × 1350, MP4, under 8 MB.

## Backgrounds (this matters most)

- **hero.png and portrait.png:** one flat, even colour behind the character, **dark grey #1a1d20**.
  No gradient, smoke, sparks, scenery or floor shadow, and nothing touching the edges except the
  body at the bottom. Flat grey lets me cut every hair cleanly; pure black loses dark hair and
  costumes, and pure white leaves halos.
- Powers that glow (Johnny's flame, Thor's lightning, Shang-Chi's rings, Cyclops's visor, Sue's
  force field) may stay in the hero shot, but keep them on the body, not filling the frame.

## Look: the same for all 29, so the site feels like one world

- **Photoreal, cinematic 3D render**, like film key art. Not cartoon, not Pixar, not anime, not a painting.
- **Lens:** 85 mm portrait look, camera at chest height, slight low angle for heroes, eye level for Peggy and Professor X.
- **Lighting:** soft key light from the front-left, a dark fill, and a **strong rim light from behind in the
  character's colour** (hex in the table). The rim light is what makes the cut-outs glow on the site.
- **Grade:** deep shadows, slightly desaturated, skin natural.
- **Costume:** the look in the table (their film costume, as Marvel last showed it).
- **Whole props in frame:** Cap's shield, Thor's weapon, Shang-Chi's rings and M'Baku's club fully inside the image.
- **Never:** text, titles, logos (no Marvel logo, no "MCU", no team logos as graphics), watermarks
  (Gemini, TikTok and others), artist signatures, borders, frames or split panels. One character per image.
  Check hands and fingers before saving.
