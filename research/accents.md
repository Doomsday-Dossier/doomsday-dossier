# Character accents: proposal for approval

One accent per character on the gunmetal ground `#0d1012`. Each comes from the character's look in the films (or the comics, where marked), with the page that describes or shows it. No wiki gives hex codes, so every hex is our own judgement of the described colour. Contrast is WCAG against `#0d1012` (all at least 4.5:1). Distinctness is CIE76 Delta E, target 12 or more: the closest pair is **sam-wilson / ghost at 13.2**.

Swatches use inline colour, which shows in most Markdown previews (VS Code, Obsidian) but not on GitHub. The hex is always given.

## Hard choices and thin sources

- **JOAQUIN TORRES** (`#8fe0a0`): thin source: the page shows the suit and says he repainted the wings and made his own costume, but its text names no colour. The hex is our judgement of the suit's green, lightened for legibility and pushed toward mint so it stays clear of Doom's emerald.
- **SUE STORM** (`#c9c0ff`): hard choice: her powers are close to colourless by design, and neither wiki names a colour for them. With the team blue given to Reed, this pale lilac is our stand-in for a force field's shimmer, not a described colour.
- **MAGNETO** (`#e05cff`): matched by eye to the described costume colour (the page mentions his red and purple suit). Purple taken over red: the red half sits on top of Cyclops's ruby, and Ian McKellen's film helmet and cape are a dark maroon (our reading; the Earth-10005 page names no colour) that would need heavy lightening.
- **NIGHTCRAWLER** (`#a493ff`): matched by eye to the described skin colour (blue), lightened for legibility and pushed toward indigo, because Mystique, Beast, Steve and Reed all hold nearby blues.
- **GAMBIT** (`#ff4fc0`): the page calls the glow purple; we take the pink-magenta end of that purple, which keeps it apart from Shuri's violet and Magneto's purple. A judgement call, not a described shade.
- **GHOST** (`#d0d6e6`): thin source: the suit page shows the costume but its text names no colour. The hex is our judgement of a pale, cool grey.
- **NAMOR** (`#2ed3c0`): matched by eye to the described skin colour (blue), nudged toward teal so it sits between Thor's sky blue and Doom's emerald. Namor himself kept a human skin tone, so this is his people's colour rather than his own.

## Clashes resolved by a second element

- **Sam Wilson** takes the white of his Wakandan-made Captain America suit, leaving the classic Captain America blue to Steve Rogers.
- **Mystique, Beast, Nightcrawler, Steve Rogers, Reed Richards** all have blue looks. They are split by depth and hue: Mystique is saturated cobalt, Steve a muted navy, Reed a bright cerulean, Beast a pale cornflower, Nightcrawler an indigo-violet.
- **Reds**: Red Guardian keeps pure red; Cyclops tilts to ruby, Peggy Carter to coral, Scott Lang to brick. Thor (cape) and John Walker (stripes) move to their lightning and shield colours.
- **Purples**: Shuri keeps the Panther Habit violet; Magneto takes the comics purple, Gambit the pink end of his charge, Sam's purple wing glow is dropped.
- **Greens**: Doom keeps emerald (fixed). Loki moves to green-gold, Joaquin Torres to a light mint green, Yelena Belova to olive, Namor to teal.
- **Pale range**: Sam (white), Ghost (cool grey), M'Baku (ivory), Bucky (steel) are kept at least 13 Delta E apart.
